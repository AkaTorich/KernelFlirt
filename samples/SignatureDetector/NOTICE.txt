Signature Detector Plugin — Third-Party Attribution
====================================================

userdb.txt — PEiD Signature Database
  Source:    PEiD community (BobSoft, various contributors)
  Mirror:    https://github.com/wolfram77web/app-peid
  License:   Public domain / community-contributed (no formal license)
  Usage:     Byte-pattern signatures for identifying packers, compilers,
             and protectors in PE executables.

This file is a community-maintained database originally distributed with
PEiD (freeware by Jibz). Signatures were contributed by the reverse
engineering community and have been freely redistributed across numerous
tools and projects (Detect It Easy, ExeInfo PE, ProtectionID, etc.).
